Frequently Asked Wheel Care Questions

How often should I align and balance my vehicle’s wheels? +

For standard highway driving, aligning and balancing is recommended every 5,000 to 10,000 kilometers (or every 6 months). However, for rocky, pothole-heavy mountain stretches (extremely common in Idukki's rugged ranges), we advise checks every 3,000 to 5,000 kilometers, or immediately if you notice the steering wheel pulling.

Why is Nitrogen air better than normal air for mountain climbs? +

Nitrogen gas contains molecules heavier than normal atmospheric air, leaking through rubber structures 3 times slower. Additionally, Nitrogen runs up to 20% cooler. Under persistent braking and friction heating on continuous steep curves, normal air spikes tyre pressure rapidly. Nitrogen maintains steady stability.

How do I check if my tyres are safe for monsoons? +

Traction safety is regulated by groove depths. All tyres carry small wear indicator nodes level with 1.6mm limits inside channels. If your tyre tread depth falls below 2mm, it will not channel rainwater efficiently, causing aquaplaning hazards. We offer free digital depth checks.
